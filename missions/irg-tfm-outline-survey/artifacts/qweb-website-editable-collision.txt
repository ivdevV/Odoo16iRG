Website editable context collision — 2026-09-10

Observed behavior:
A Portal student with a draft outline and no TFM convocation reached step 1,
but the template rendered "Este cuestionario está disponible únicamente en
modo lectura" and no questions.

Root cause:
The controller exposed the business permission as the generic QWeb key
`editable`. Odoo Website 16 supplies that same key for website-editor state;
for a Portal student it is false and overrides the questionnaire flag.
