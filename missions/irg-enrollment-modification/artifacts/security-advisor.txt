- No Stripe secrets, no auth archive, no historical delete, no deploy config

Controls required (implementation conditions)

1. Every mutating public method (`action_create_request`, `action_approve_academic`,
   `action_approve_finance`, `action_refuse`, `action_retry_pdf`) must call
   `has_group()` before any `sudo()`. View `groups=` is UX only.
2. Academic group is a new `irg_enrollment_modification.group_academic`. Finance
   uses existing `account.group_account_invoice`. Settings (`base.group_system`)
   may do both. Portal has no ACL on the request or wizard.
3. `sudo()` is limited to the write of `op.student.course` (marked academic
   fields) and `sale.order.payment_mode_id` (and SOL `x_studio_modalidad` when
   present). Chatter must run as the acting user.
4. Underscore helpers (`_check_academic_user`, `_write_academic_fields`,
   `_write_payment_mode`, `_fill_docx`, `_convert_to_pdf`) are not RPC-safe
   by naming; do not expose a public `sudo()`-backed helper that accepts
   arbitrary student/order ids from the client without going through the
   request record and group check.
5. Negative tests: Academic `with_user` cannot `action_approve_finance`;
   accountant cannot `action_create_request` / `action_approve_academic`.
   After AccessError, course and payment_mode are unchanged.
6. No deletion of historical sale orders or enrollments. Academic write is
   in-place on the chosen `op.student.course`. Finance refuse does not
   revert academic writes (documented).
7. Template lives in the addon; no credentials in TestSprite `projectPath`
   (module dir only).

Residual risk

- In-place course/batch write can hit OpenEduCat uniqueness
  `(student, course, batch)`; must raise ValidationError, not swallow.
- `payment_mode_id` change does not rewrite invoices; out of scope and must
  stay that way.

[YES] Reason: Group-gated mutating methods, narrow sudo after has_group, no portal ACL, negative RPC tests, and no historical delete or payment-provider secrets. Residual uniqueness collision is a ValidationError, not a privilege bypass.
